# The data licences (November 17th 2026)

John's subscriptions to three Rail Data Marketplace products were
approved on November 17th 2026. He shared each product's "Data Sharing
Agreement" as a PDF; Claude Code compared the three word for word. The
main terms are the same in all three; the differences are in the last
column of the table.

## The same in all three

1. **Free:** "Licence fee type: Open. The Data Consumer can access the
   Licensed Data without charge." No fair-use limit or charges stated.
2. **Term:** one year, renewing automatically each year; John can end it
   with a week's notice.
3. **What we may do:** "The raw data may be made freely available or
   otherwise distributed to third parties." Recipients: "All registered
   users".
4. **Credit (clause 3.3.1):** we must "give an appropriate credit to the
   Data Publisher" (the Rail Delivery Group) "as the source of the
   Licensed Data", "in any reasonable manner, but not in a way that
   suggests the Data Publisher or its licensors endorse" us.
5. **Accuracy (clauses 3.3.2, 3.5, 7.2):** the data comes "as is"; when
   passing it on, we use reasonable efforts not to make it inaccurate or
   incomplete, and we tell the publisher of any faults we notice
   (3.3.3).
6. **After the term:** we "may retain any data which has been received".

## What this means for Trainchecker (for John to confirm)

1. **Credit line:** name both, without suggesting endorsement. Draft:
   "Train information from National Rail, supplied by the Rail Delivery
   Group through the Rail Data Marketplace. Trainchecker isn't connected
   with or endorsed by either." Plus a line that times can change and
   aren't guaranteed. Wording agreed with John at the mock-ups (house
   style §8).
2. **"UK only" for the incidents and disruptions data:** Vercel's functions run in
   Washington, D.C. by default (Vercel's own documentation, read November
   17th 2026). Set the project's function region to London (`lhr1`) so
   the incidents data is fetched and handled in the UK.
3. **Reselling:** not relevant; Trainchecker is free and doesn't sell
   data.
