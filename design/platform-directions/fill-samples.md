# Platform directions: entrances on the other side (samples, November 24th 2026)

Draft wording, for John. "Now" is what the site says; "would be" adds the entrances it leaves out, using the
platform links (the same table as Connections). Not live. Illustration only: from the map data, not checked
at the stations. Stations with John's own corrections (Reading, Paddington, Twyford, Bath Spa) are left as they are.

## A. Over the footbridge (396 answers)

1. **Lea Bridge, platform 1**
   - Now: "Platform 1 is right by the Argall Way entrance."
   - Would be: "Platform 1 is right by the Argall Way entrance. From the Rigg Approach entrance by platform 2, go over the footbridge to platform 1."

2. **Accrington, platform 1**
   - Now: "Platform 1 is right by the car park entrance. From the entrance by platform 2, go up to the footbridge and cross to platform 1."
   - Would be: "Platform 1 is right by the car park entrance. From the entrance by platform 2, go up to the footbridge and cross to platform 1. From the Scaitcliffe Street entrance by platform 2, go over the footbridge to platform 1."

## B. Through the subway (128 answers)

3. **Anniesland, platform 3**
   - Now: "Platform 3 is right by the entrance by the station building."
   - Would be: "Platform 3 is right by the entrance by the station building. From the Herschell Street entrance by platform 2, go through the subway to platform 3."

4. **Elephant & Castle, platform 2**
   - Now: "From the Ash Avenue or Elephant Road entrance, go down to the subway by platform 4 and come up on platform 2."
   - Would be: "From the Ash Avenue or Elephant Road entrance, go down to the subway by platform 4 and come up on platform 2. From the Hampton Street entrance by platform 1, go through the subway to platform 2."

## D. On the same level, along the platforms (34 answers)

## E. The same island platform (25 answers)

8. **Bolton, platform 4**
   - Now: "Platform 4 is right by the Trinity Street and university entrances. From the Newport Street entrance, go up to the footbridge by platform 5 and cross to platform 4."
   - Would be: "Platform 4 is right by the Trinity Street and university entrances. From the Newport Street entrance, go up to the footbridge by platform 5 and cross to platform 4. From the Station Street entrance by platform 5, platform 4 is just across the platform."

## G. A big station with a concourse (11 answers)

   - Would be: "From the King's Cross Square, Euston Road or Pancras Road entrance, cross the concourse to platform 0. From the Kings Boulevard entrance by platform 9, go over the footbridge or round through the concourse to platform 0."
