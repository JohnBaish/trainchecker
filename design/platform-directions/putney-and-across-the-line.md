## 2. The rule for everywhere else

In platform N's own answers, an entrance or exit is never called "the entrance by platform N" or "the entrance nearest platform N". Where its way in crosses the line (a footbridge or subway: 62 of the 63 guide answers), it's "the entrance across the line" ("the exit across the line" in Exits). Otherwise it's "the other entrance" beside one more, "another entrance" beside two or more, or "the entrance" alone.
