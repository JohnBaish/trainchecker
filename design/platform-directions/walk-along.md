# "Right there" only within 60 metres (November 27th 2026)

John's rule: up to 60 metres "right there"; over 60 "a short walk along … on the same level"; over 120 "a walk along".
Where a platform has an exit of its own, that one is named as right there. The metres are walking distances along the
map's paths (OpenStreetMap), not exact. Every answer that changed, Exits and the platform guide, live `main` before
and this branch after. Remade by `node --experimental-strip-types` on the script in the session notes.
