# "Over the footbridge": wording drafts (November 21st 2026)

Claude's drafts, for John to edit. The words go into the existing platform sentence in Connections,
just after the platform you want, as in John's own example ("…you'll be wanting platform 1, over
the footbridge, where…"). Where a label like 9B or 11A isn't on the map, the map's platform 9 or 11
is used; where neither is, nothing is added.

## On real boards (saved at 2.55pm on November 21st 2026)

1. Ivybridge to Newton Abbot, for Torquay (Newton Abbot: 1/2 | 3, by a bridge):
   "You're coming into platform 3 and you'll want platform 1, over the footbridge, where your train
   will be showing on the departure board as the Paignton train."
2. Twyford to Reading, for Swindon (Reading: 12/13 to 8/9, by a bridge):
   "You're coming into platform 12 and you'll want platform 8, over the footbridge, where your train
   will be showing on the departure board as the Carmarthen train."
3. Twyford to Reading, for London Paddington, two trains (12 to 14, and 12 to 11A):
   "You're coming into platform 12 and you'll want platform 14 for the 3.29 Elizabeth Line train,
   or platform 11A for the 3.29 fast train, both over the footbridge."

## The other kinds (illustration only: real stations, made-up trains)

4. A subway (Bath Spa, 1 to 2): "…you'll want platform 2, through the subway, where…"
5. Either (Clapham Junction, some pairs): "…you'll want platform 13, over the footbridge or
   through the subway, where…"
6. The same island (Reading, 9 to 8): "…you'll want platform 8, just across the platform, where…"
7. On the level but not the same island (Reading, 4 to 7; Oxford, 1 to 3): "…you'll want
   platform 7, on the same level, where…"
8. Out of the station (Reading West, 1 to 2): "…you'll want platform 2, which means going out of
   the station and round by the road, where…"
9. A level crossing (Midgham, 1 to 2): "…you'll want platform 2, across the level crossing,
   where…"
10. A bridge, or a walk round on the level (Paddington, some pairs): "…you'll want platform 1,
    over the footbridge or round through the concourse, where…"
11. Over or under, the map doesn't say which, or no walk found: nothing added.
